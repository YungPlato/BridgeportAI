Common Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

Absolutely not. For our Agency services, we handle 100% of the technical heavy lifting. For our Studio classes, we teach you how to use user-friendly AI tools that require zero coding knowledge.

Why are you cheaper than other agencies?

Efficiency. Most agencies charge for hours of manual coding and copywriting. We use advanced AI to do 80% of that work in minutes, then spend our time polishing the design. We pass those savings directly to you.

How long does a website take?

Our typical turnaround for a Launch Kit website is 3-5 business days. Once we have your info, we move fast.

Is there a monthly fee for the website?

For the Launch Kit ($499), it is a one-time design fee. You will only pay for your own domain hosting (usually ~$10-20/month) which we help you set up in your own name so you own it forever.
